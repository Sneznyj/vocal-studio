Vocal analysis from a recording

Upload a vocal take, for example from REAPER, and get the same report as after singing in the app. With a song from My songs, the take is compared to the original melody and its place in the song is found automatically.

Voice recordingVoice only, no backing track: wav, mp3, m4a…

What the analysis shows

  • Intonation: how many notes are in tune, where you go flat or sharp.
  • Rhythm and stability: entries, long notes, vibrato.
  • Breathing and range: where you run out of air, which notes are hard.
  • Tips with exercises: exactly what to practise to sing better.
