How to record your voice so the processing sounds better
• Wear headphones: the backing track will not leak into the microphone.• 15–20 cm from the microphone — no boomy lows.• A quiet room with furniture or a blanket — less echo.• Level up to the yellow zone of the meter, but not the red.

How to process your voice online

  • Record your voice with the microphone (over a backing track if you like, with reverb in your headphones) or upload a ready file: wav, mp3, m4a, ogg, flac.
  • Pick a preset — Velvet, Pop Hit, Ballad, Rock, Rap, Cathedral — or an effect: T-Pain style autotune, radio, telephone, robot, giant.
  • Compare before and after with one button: loudness is matched so you hear the real difference.
  • Download the result: MP3 320 or 24-bit WAV, the voice alone or mixed with the backing track, at −14 LUFS like streaming services.

What the processing does

Every preset is a chain a sound engineer would build by hand: cleanup (room noise reduction, de-esser, silence between phrases), key-aware autotune, EQ, compressor, tube warmth, doubling, tempo-synced echo and reverb. In Fine tuning every module can be switched on separately, and your own settings can be saved as a preset.

Frequently asked questions

How do I autotune my voice online for free?

Upload or record your voice and choose any preset — autotune is already on and follows the key of the song. For the rapper-style effect choose "Autotune effect".

Can I put my vocals over a backing track?

Yes: upload the backing track together with your voice or record over it. You can shift the voice against the track, and the export mixes them into one file.

Where does my recording go?

Nowhere: recording and processing happen in your browser, on your device. Projects are saved there as well.

Is it really free?

Yes, with no sign-up and no limits: all presets and exports are available right away.
