Microphone needed

Sound is analysed right in your browser and never uploaded. The browser will ask for permission once.

  • Wear headphones when singing over a track so the music doesn't leak into the microphone.
  • Audio interface with two inputs (Scarlett, etc.): the voice is found on either input automatically, and you can pick the channel in Settings.
  • Level: loud parts should reach the yellow zone of the meter, but not the red.

How to check whether you sing in tune

  • Allow the microphone and sing a note on "ah". The needle shows the deviation in cents: 100 cents is a semitone, anything within ±15 is in tune.
  • Below, your pitch line is drawn over the notes: you can see where you scoop up into a note, where you go flat at the end of a phrase and what your vibrato looks like.
  • Choose the key of your song — the notes of the scale light up and the off-key ones stand out.
  • "Record and analyse" saves your singing and shows a detailed report: intonation, stability, breathing and range.

Instrument tuner

Tuning a guitar or a violin? Pick the instrument at the top of the page: the tuner recognises the string and tells you whether to tighten or loosen it. There are tuners for guitar (standard, Drop D, Open G and more), bass up to 5 and 6 strings, ukulele, violin, viola, cello, mandolin, balalaika, domra, banjo, 7-string guitar and a chromatic tuner.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I sing on pitch?

Sing into the tuner: a green needle near the centre means the note is clean. If your pitch line stays below the notes you are singing flat, above — sharp.

What are cents?

One hundredth of a semitone. Experienced singers stay within ±10–15 cents; more than 30 cents off is audible even to untrained listeners.

Does a phone microphone work?

Yes. The tuner runs in the browser on computers and phones as long as it can use the microphone.
