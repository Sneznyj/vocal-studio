Warm-up: scales and arpeggios

The piano plays the chord and the notes of the exercise, and you sing along. Each repetition is a semitone higher. Press Record a take to get a report.

10 repetitions: from C3 to E4Default range — take the range test to fit your voice.

Exercises

  • Five-note scale 1-2-3-4-5-4-3-2-1 on "ma" — the basis of any warm-up.
  • Arpeggio 1-3-5-8 on "ah" — leaps along the chord, accuracy and an easy top.
  • Thirds 1-2-3-2-1 on "mi" — a gentle warm-up.
  • Octave leap — a confident top without tension.

The piano plays the chord and the notes with you, each repetition a semitone higher. After the range test, the range adapts to your voice.
